About

A solo developer service for one stack

AppContinuity is a solo developer service. The work is fixed-scope RevenueCat integration and sandbox acceptance testing for founders who already have a React app wrapped with Capacitor, people signing in with Supabase Auth, and subscriptions selling on the web through Stripe.

The goal is simple to say and hard to keep: paid access should give the same answer on web, iPhone and Android. That means wiring RevenueCat into the Capacitor app, mapping Supabase users to RevenueCat customers, connecting Stripe web subscriptions to the same entitlement, and handing over a written sandbox suite — purchase, renewal, cancellation, refund, restore and account switching — rather than stopping at a single successful tap.

Work happens on a branch in the client’s repository, using access they grant and can revoke. Their RevenueCat, App Store Connect, Google Play, Stripe and Supabase accounts stay theirs. AppContinuity is independent and is not affiliated with RevenueCat, Stripe, Supabase, Apple or Google.

Only one Integration runs at a time. Acceptance is sandbox: if the agreed sandbox acceptance tests do not pass because of the integration work, the work continues at no extra fee or the remaining half of the Integration fee is refunded. That guarantee does not cover App Store or Play approval, production purchases, or work outside the written scope.

This site publishes technical notes on the same stack, including how a Stripe web subscriber can still hit a paywall in a Capacitor iPhone app when identity and entitlements drift. There are no invented client stories here. Public prices for the Continuity Audit, Integration and Continuity Care are on the pricing section.
